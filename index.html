ABOUT NILOTES

Food as a bridge between heritage and opportunity.

The Nilotes Initiative focuses on gastronomy, food systems, cultural heritage and youth development in a fragile, post-conflict environment.

The initiative responds to the need to professionalize and promote South Sudanese cuisine while contributing to food security, economic opportunities and peacebuilding through food.

OUR MISSION

“To rediscover, preserve, promote and innovate South Sudanese cuisine as a vehicle for cultural pride, food-system resilience, youth empowerment and sustainable economic development in South Sudan.”

South Sudan Food Week

A National Platform for Culinary Heritage & Sustainable Gastronomy

About the Event

South Sudan Food Week is a national platform celebrating local cuisine, agriculture, and culture. Inspired by models such as Cairo Food Week, it showcases South Sudanese talent alongside regional and international chefs, promotes local produce, and creates immersive experiences that blend heritage with modern hospitality.

Objectives

To place South Sudanese food and chefs on the regional and global gastronomy map, strengthen local value chains, reduce import dependence, and position the country as a destination for authentic culinary experiences.

Mission

We celebrate our diverse culinary heritage while advancing sustainable gastronomy through the use of local ingredients, short supply chains, and responsible practices as the future of hospitality. Through chef collaborations, markets, tastings, and dialogues, the event supports local producers, elevates emerging talent, and builds resilient food systems that nourish communities and power the hospitality sector.
